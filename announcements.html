Announcements

Recently accepted papers, public preprints, publications, and important updates for the JUMP community. We will add other relevant papers and resources as they become available.

Entries are listed newest first within each section. Dates identify the reported milestone—acceptance, publication, preprint posting, or an update—not the date an entry was added here. Conference editions can differ from the year of acceptance.

Recent papers

2026-10-15 — JUMP-lite accepted to WACV 2027

Conference paper · Accepted October 15, 2026 · WACV 2027 · Public preprint available

JUMP-lite: Compact, reproducible benchmarking of cell representations has been accepted to WACV 2027. JUMP-lite provides a compact subset of JUMP Cell Painting for developing and comparing cell-image representations. The paper also introduces Nahual, an open-source framework for reproducible model deployment.

Read the public preprint, explore the JUMP-lite data and code, or try Nahual.

2025-06-09 — cp_measure accepted to CODEML at ICML 2025

Workshop paper · Accepted June 9, 2025 · CODEML at ICML 2025 (non-archival) · Public preprint available

cp_measure: API-first feature extraction for image-based profiling workflows was accepted to the CODEML workshop at ICML 2025. The Python library brings CellProfiler’s core measurement capabilities into a modular, API-first tool, making morphological feature extraction easier to integrate into automated image-analysis workflows.

The public preprint was posted on July 1, 2025. Explore the code and documentation.
