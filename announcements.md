# Announcements

Recently accepted papers, public preprints, publications, and important updates.

## Recent papers

### 2026-10-15 — JUMP-lite accepted to WACV 2027

**Conference paper · WACV 2027 · Public preprint available**

*JUMP-lite: Compact, reproducible benchmarking of cell representations* has been
accepted to WACV 2027. JUMP-lite provides a compact subset of JUMP Cell Painting
for developing and comparing cell-image representations. The paper also
introduces Nahual, an open-source framework for reproducible model deployment.

Read the [public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07632), explore the
[JUMP-lite data and code](https://github.com/afermg/JUMP_lite), or try
[Nahual](https://github.com/afermg/nahual).

### 2025-06-09 — cp_measure accepted to CODEML at ICML 2025

**Workshop paper · Accepted June 9, 2025 · CODEML at ICML 2025 · Public preprint available**

*cp_measure: API-first feature extraction for image-based profiling workflows*
was accepted to the CODEML workshop at ICML 2025. The Python library brings
CellProfiler's core measurement capabilities into a modular, API-first tool,
making morphological feature extraction easier to integrate into automated
image-analysis workflows.

The [public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01163) was posted on July 1, 2025.
Explore the [code and documentation](https://github.com/afermg/cp_measure).

### 2025-06-04 — copairs: evaluating profile strength and similarity

### 2024-12-16 — MotiVE: drug–target interaction prediction
