# Announcements

Recently accepted papers, public preprints, publications, and important updates.

## Recent papers

### 2026-10-15 — JUMP-lite accepted to WACV 2027

**Accepted conference paper · WACV 2027**

JUMP-lite provides a compact subset of JUMP Cell Painting for developing and
comparing cell-image representations. The paper also introduces Nahual, an
open-source framework for reproducible model deployment.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07632) ·
[GitHub (JUMP-lite)](https://github.com/afermg/JUMP_lite) ·
[GitHub (Nahual)](https://github.com/afermg/nahual)

### 2025-09-15 — OASIS: multi-omics for chemical safety assessment

**Published · Toxicological Sciences**

The OASIS Consortium combines Cell Painting, transcriptomics, and proteomics
across cell models to improve chemical safety assessment. The initiative aims
to make safety testing more predictive of human biology while reducing animal use.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/toxsci/kfaf128)

### 2025-08-07 — Morphological map of under- and overexpression of genes in human cells

**Published · Nature Methods**

The JUMP Cell Painting genetic dataset captures the effects of perturbing
15,243 human genes through CRISPR knockouts and gene overexpression. Its
single-cell images and morphological profiles support exploration of gene
functions and relationships.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02753-9)

### 2025-06-09 — cp_measure accepted to CODEML at ICML 2025

**Accepted workshop paper · CODEML at ICML 2025**

cp_measure brings CellProfiler's core measurement capabilities into a modular,
API-first Python library for morphological feature extraction. Its public
preprint was posted on July 1, 2025.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01163) ·
[GitHub](https://github.com/afermg/cp_measure)

### 2025-06-04 — copairs: evaluating profile strength and similarity

**Published · Nature Communications**

An information-retrieval framework using mean average precision to measure
phenotypic activity and consistency.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-60306-2) ·
[GitHub](https://github.com/cytomining/copairs)

### 2025-03-03 — Reproducible image-based profiling with Pycytominer

**Published · Nature Methods**

Pycytominer is an open-source Python package for processing single-cell
measurements into profiles for downstream analysis. It provides reproducible
bioinformatics steps for image-based profiling workflows.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02611-8)

### 2024-12-16 — MotiVE: drug–target interaction prediction

**Published · NeurIPS 2024, Datasets and Benchmarks Track**

A drug–target interaction graph combining Cell Painting profiles and known
relationships to benchmark predictions for unseen compounds and genes.

[Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/fdb3fa770c2e0ecbb4b7dc7083ef5be9-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html) ·
[GitHub](https://github.com/carpenter-singh-lab/2024_Arevalo_NeurIPS_MotiVE)

### 2024-12-05 — Cell Painting: a decade of discovery and innovation in cellular imaging

**Published review · Nature Methods**

A review of a decade of Cell Painting advances, from assay protocols and image
analysis to biological applications. It covers feature extraction, quality
control, batch correction, and integration with other omics data.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02528-8)

### 2024-09-02 — Cell Painting Gallery: an open resource for image-based profiling

**Published · Nature Methods**

Cell Painting Gallery provides public image datasets for image-based profiling,
including JUMP. The resource supports data discovery and reuse for biological
research and computational method development.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02399-z)

### 2024-08-02 — Batch correction for Cell Painting

**Published · Nature Communications**

A benchmark of batch-correction methods for integrating JUMP Cell Painting
profiles across laboratories and microscopes.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50613-5) ·
[GitHub](https://github.com/carpenter-singh-lab/2023_Arevalo_BatchCorrection)
